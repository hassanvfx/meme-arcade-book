# Evidence sheet

## Question

Which architecture contracts can be reused across iOS products, and which responsibilities must remain product-specific and private?

## Public evidence

- `ios-framework` locked tandem-app/package/test structure.
- `ios-storage`, `receipe-app`, GamePlayer, Pushscheduler, and `rezona-api` locked responsibility boundaries and quality/evidence practices.

## Approved generalized MemeArcade evidence

- State, persistence, client, player host, WebView policy, scheduler, and observability are reusable responsibility categories.
- Product data, content, routes, business rules, and private implementation remain at the application boundary.

No private package graph, source, module, API, endpoint, payload, catalog, account data, notification copy, metric, security policy, or business logic is approved for publication.

## Claims requiring approval

- Any exact private extraction plan, package layout, or implementation claim.

## Planned diagram

Domain/policy → infrastructure adapters → feature host → application composition, with private product concerns held outside reusable contracts.
