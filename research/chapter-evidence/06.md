# Evidence sheet

## Question

How can an iOS team observe authorized client traffic, derive a safe client contract, and report the limit of what the observation proves?

## Public evidence

- `rezona-api` locked at `18d700c16ae689588519cd75315b2a7175f2db6b`.
- Its README separates attributed claims, observed lower bounds, conditional scenarios, and exploratory model output.
- The repository documents bounded/retried collection, checkpointed local raw captures, aggregation/validation tests, public manifests, and Git-ignored raw data.
- Its disclosure limits authenticated/state-changing work to the reader's own authorized account and forbids committing a local token.

## Approved generalized MemeArcade evidence

- Native code retains trust and routing policy at the device boundary.

No private capture, endpoint, header, cookie, token, payload, backend claim, source excerpt, or metric is approved for publication.
