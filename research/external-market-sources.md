# Approved framing

The phrase “$3B MemeArcade” is an editorial label for the broader AI-gaming market, not a literal valuation, revenue figure, or funding claim about MemeArcade, the App. The opening chapter must state that distinction before discussing the iOS case study.

# Market sources

- Mordor Intelligence, “Generative AI in Game Environment and Narrative Design”: projects a global 2026 market size of USD 3.05 billion. This is the primary source for the rounded $3B market framing.
- Research and Markets, “AI in Games Market Report 2026”: estimates USD 3.4 billion for the broader global AI-in-games market in 2026. Use as corroboration, not as a figure to combine with the first estimate.
- Axios, June 1 2026: Sekai announced a USD 20 million Series A.
- Fortune, May 5 2026: Astrocade announced USD 56 million in new funding.
- Business Insider, March 5 2026: the Gizmo/Atma Sciences team joined Meta's Superintelligence Labs; the article says the deal terms were not disclosed. Use only as a talent-market signal, not as funding evidence.

# Publication rule

All dated market claims must include their source link and publication date. Do not add valuations, totals, or market-size estimates together unless the sources use the same metric and scope.
